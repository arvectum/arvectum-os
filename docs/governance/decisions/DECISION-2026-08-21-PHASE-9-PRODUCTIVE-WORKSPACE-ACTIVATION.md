## 1. Decision

**APPROVED — Phase 9 `Productive Workspace & Daily Operations` becomes `Active` for the bounded internal owner-operated scope.**

The owner explicitly approved proceeding step-by-step with the proposed Productive Workspace program and using the resulting Workspace in real work.

The decision responds to a confirmed post-M8 usability gap: Arvectum OS has a persistent runtime and proven private browser workspace, but the current UI remains primarily a technical/reference presentation of governed records, exact identities, versions, executions, evidence and preflight state rather than a productive daily organizational workbench.

## 2. Approved outcome

Approved Phase 9 outcome:

> **Make Arvectum OS the normal daily private work interface for the owner of ООО «Арвектум» for attention management, discovery, context understanding, governed decisions/actions and work across real products, without requiring terminal, GitHub or knowledge of internal identifiers for ordinary work.**

The existing P4/P7 UI remains valid diagnostic/reference/recovery evidence and may continue to exist. Phase 9 is not approval to expand that adapter indefinitely into a long-lived application by default.

## 3. Approved sequencing

The approved critical path is:

`P9.01 real operator jobs → P9.02 application architecture spike → R29 → P9.03 application shell → P9.04 My Work → P9.05 human-friendly Records/Documents/Knowledge/search → P9.06 Executions/Decisions/governed actions → R30 → M9-alpha → P9.07 product surfaces → P9.08 AI Copilot → P9.09 activity/notifications → P9.10 company composition → R31 → P9.11 daily-use dogfooding → R32 + code-health gate → P9.12 / M9`.

Technology selection is intentionally deferred to P9.02. If a frontend framework, BFF/API/session topology or durable projection technology becomes materially constraining or long-lived, an ADR is required before material reliance.

## 4. Platform / product boundary

Approved platform scope includes domain-neutral operator capabilities such as:

- human-readable workspace navigation and organizational context;
- attention/work projections over governed state;
- discovery/search presentation;
- Records/Documents/Knowledge/Execution context presentation;
- authority-safe governed action composition;
- source-grounded AI assistance/proposals;
- product-surface composition mechanism where reuse is justified.

Product-specific schemas, workflows, dispositions, approval rules, reports, prompts, business decisions and UX remain product-owned and may enter Workspace only through explicit Product Contract/extension boundaries.

## 5. Authority / security boundary

This decision does not authorize the UI, AI, presentation state or product extension to mint:

- Authorization;
- Organizational Authority;
- Data Governance permission;
- Consequential Approval.

Consequential canonical change remains through Governed Execution. Browser/client state is never a security or authority boundary by itself. Existing least-privilege, Organization-scope, provenance, replay-safety, minimization and retention invariants remain binding.

## 6. Intermediate milestone

The owner-approved intermediate milestone is:

**`M9-alpha — Usable Internal Workspace`**.

M9-alpha is achieved only after the owner can use a normal private browser application to open a useful home page, see work requiring attention, find and understand real organizational objects, inspect a real execution/decision and perform at least one bounded real governed interaction without terminal/GitHub/internal-ID knowledge for ordinary steps.

M9-alpha is not a lifecycle, Production, support or public-interface transition.

## 7. Explicit non-claims

This activation does not establish:

- external/customer Production;
- public SaaS;
- Stable Product Contract;
- Active Platform Capability;
- public/stable API, SDK, browser/session contract or support matrix;
- multi-Organization validation beyond already proven scope;
- SLA/support/certification;
- AI authority or autonomous final consequential approval.

## 8. Current canonical action

> **P9.01 — Real operator jobs-to-be-done + acceptance journeys.**

Long-lived application technology selection and broad implementation must wait until P9.01 fixes the exact jobs and acceptance evidence.
