## Decision

The Owner of Arvectum OS explicitly approves RFC-0001 version `0.9.0` as the final proposed text and authorizes its transition, without substantive architectural changes, to stable version `1.0.0` with status `Accepted`.

## Scope of Approval

This decision approves the normative architecture described by RFC-0001, including:

- the three architectural laws;
- the five Kernel semantic primitives and provisional Kernel metamodel constraint;
- Canonical Record authority modes;
- Product Experiment, Platform Capability and Product Contract boundaries;
- capability lifecycle and operational-readiness requirements for `Active` capabilities;
- commercial-commitment integrity rules;
- decision-authority and architectural-exception invariants;
- structural security, privacy, isolation, sovereignty and portability invariants;
- scoped conformance and architectural fitness tests;
- the formal RFC approval process.

Informative guidance remains informative as defined by RFC-0001 and is not converted into an immutable commercial roadmap or implementation commitment by this approval.

## Rationale

RFC-0001 has completed repeated cross-review from founder, executive, architecture, technology, product, commercial, finance, operations, security, privacy, legal, data, AI, risk, compliance, customer-success and organizational perspectives.

The resulting architecture is considered sufficiently stable to serve as the first accepted architectural contract beneath Constitution `1.2.0`. Further detail belongs in subsequent RFCs, ADRs, policies, product contracts and bounded experiments rather than continued expansion of RFC-0001.

## Consequences

1. RFC-0001 shall be published as `1.0.0 Accepted` without substantive architectural changes from approved proposal `0.9.0`.
2. The RFC Index shall identify RFC-0001 `1.0.0` as `Accepted`.
3. Future Arvectum OS architectural, product-contract and implementation decisions must conform to Constitution `1.2.0` and RFC-0001 `1.0.0` unless superseded through an approved governance process.
4. The proposed Decision Authority Policy remains a separate governance decision and is not implicitly accepted by this decision.
5. RFC-0002 and later documents may refine areas explicitly left provisional by RFC-0001 but may not contradict its accepted normative rules without an approved superseding RFC.
