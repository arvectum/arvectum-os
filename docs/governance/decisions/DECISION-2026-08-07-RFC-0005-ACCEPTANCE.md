## Acceptance scope

The approval makes RFC-0005 binding, once canonically published as `Accepted 1.0.0`, for domain-neutral Governed Execution and Workflow semantics including:

- Workflow identity, immutable versioning and effective-version resolution;
- Execution Context lifecycle and governance-significant transitions;
- actor, trigger and Organization scope;
- operation and side-effect semantics;
- consequential input and Product Contract version pinning;
- authentication, authorization, Organizational Authority, data-governance, validation and approval separation;
- canonical mutation boundaries;
- deterministic and AI-mediated execution constraints;
- idempotency, retry and uncertain-outcome handling;
- waiting, suspension, resumption, timeout and deadline semantics;
- parent/child execution boundaries;
- external authority preservation;
- failure, cancellation, compensation and partial completion;
- output/artifact classification;
- workflow evolution and in-flight migration;
- proportional reconstructability evidence;
- semantic portability and scoped conformance.

This approval does not pre-empt RFC-0006 Event/Provenance/Observability semantics or RFC-0007 Memory/Knowledge/Governed Learning semantics, does not activate any Platform Capability, and does not establish operational-readiness, SLA, support or commercial commitments.
