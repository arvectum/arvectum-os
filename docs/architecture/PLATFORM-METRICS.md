# Arvectum OS Platform Metrics

Status: `Informative`
Version: `0.5.0`
Updated: `2026-08-07`
Architecture basis: `RFC-0001 v0.8.0`
Constitution basis: `1.2.0`

## Purpose

This document defines initial evidence for whether Arvectum OS creates measurable organizational and platform leverage.

Metrics do not override the Constitution, accepted RFCs or product commitments.

## Measurement Principle

The platform is valuable only when it improves outcomes while preserving organizational control, security and portability.

It must not be evaluated primarily by service count, abstraction count, RFC count, lines of code or infrastructure complexity.

## Product Delivery and Experimentation

- time from product need to bounded Product Experiment;
- platform-caused delivery delay;
- experiments with owner, review date and exit path;
- experiments promoted, contained or retired on time;
- product-local experiments burdened by unnecessary platform ceremony.

## Contract Proportionality

- time required to create a minimal Provisional Product Contract;
- platform interactions without a declared contract;
- unnecessary contract fields or controls;
- delay attributable to ceremony rather than risk control.

## Decision Authority and Governance

- material decisions with a named decision authority;
- material decisions self-approved without explicit delegated authority;
- lifecycle transitions without canonical approval references;
- architectural exceptions without expiry, review date or exit plan;
- known gaps accepted by an authority lacking the affected scope;
- decisions unnecessarily escalated to the owner despite valid delegation;
- overdue governance-policy reviews;
- time from proposal to decision by risk class.

## Kernel Metamodel

- implementations declaring provisional Kernel metamodel assumptions;
- irreversible schemas or public contracts created before RFC-0002;
- migration boundaries preserved between plausible metamodels;
- cost of migrating provisional Event, Relationship or Execution Context representations;
- incompatible metamodel interpretations discovered across products.

## Conformance Semantics

- Conformance Statements recording lifecycle, environment and maturity separately;
- `Production` incorrectly used as a lifecycle state;
- limited-scope conformance represented as full-platform conformance;
- unjustified `Not Applicable` declarations;
- manual controls without review or replacement conditions;
- material exceptions approved by their proposer;
- overdue conformance reviews.

## Canonical Authority and External Systems

- Canonical Records without authority mode;
- competing sources of truth;
- Governed Replicas outside freshness targets;
- synchronization conflicts without a declared rule;
- external-system outages without defined behavior.

## Reuse and Platform Gravity

- real consumers per capability;
- time and cost to implement a second consumer;
- duplicated validated shared capabilities;
- capability adoption, bypass and abandonment;
- exploratory areas treated as funded roadmap commitments.

## Economics

- capability responsibility and support cost;
- integration cost versus product-specific or external alternatives;
- measurable operating cost or risk avoided;
- de-platformization and retirement cost;
- cost of replacing a model or technology adapter.

## Security, Privacy and Isolation

- unauthorized access and tenant-isolation incidents;
- least-privilege review coverage;
- sensitive operations with attributable audit records;
- overdue retention or deletion actions;
- data-minimization exceptions.

## Organizational Control and Portability

- time to produce a governed export;
- export completeness for identities, versions, relationships and provenance;
- tested migration success;
- deletion requests completed under policy;
- organizational assets dependent on inaccessible proprietary representations.

## Baselines and Anti-gaming

Targets require an operational baseline and must identify owner, data source, review period, rationale and harmful incentives.

Metrics must not encourage premature platformization, unnecessary approvals, excessive data collection, retention of failed capabilities, or relabeling product experiments as platform reuse.

## Review Cadence

During product validation, metrics should be reviewed at meaningful pilot, delivery, governance, migration or capability milestones rather than through heavy fixed reporting.
